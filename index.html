07 Business Intelligence

Chicken Republic Stockout Analysis Dashboard

A production-grade 5-page Power BI dashboard analysing stockout patterns, demand behaviour, and stocking efficiency across 5 Chicken Republic branches in Lagos (Ajah, Lekki, Ikeja, Surulere, and Yaba). The dashboard was built to give operations and supply chain teams actionable visibility into where, when, and why stockouts occur and what it costs the business.

Key findings: an overall stockout rate of 0.22, ₦12M in total lost revenue, and 401 stockout days across the dataset. Ajah is the worst-performing branch (0.29), Fried Chicken (Full) is the most frequently stocked-out item, and stockouts peak between hours 19–20. Saturday drives the highest sales volume, and promotions marginally reduce stockout rates (0.21 vs 0.22 without promo). Suggested opening stock of 119.59 units with a stock adjustment of -6.28 per cycle.

The 5 pages cover: Executive Stockout Summary, Stockout Overview, Demand Patterns, External Drivers (weather and promotions), and Stocking Efficiency, each with interactive slicers for branch, item, and date.

08 Business Intelligence

HVAC Energy Efficiency & Performance Analytics

An end-to-end Power BI dashboard transforming complex IoT sensor logs from a commercial HVAC chiller plant into actionable energy insights, helping facility managers identify inefficiencies, reduce operational costs, and optimise building cooling performance. Built on a star schema data model with custom DAX measures and a dark-mode visual layout.

Key metrics: 0.48 kW/RT average efficiency, 1.73M kWh total energy consumption, 520.94 RT average building load, and a 10% inefficient rate across 14K logged intervals with anomaly spikes reaching 1.51 kW/RT. Building demand peaks sharply at 08:00 AM and ramps down after 08:00 PM, exposing clear off-hour setback opportunities.

The 4 pages cover: Executive Summary (baseline KPIs and multi-year trend analysis), Weather Impact (efficiency vs. outdoor temperature and humidity), Time Pattern Analysis (hourly, daily, and weekday/weekend load behaviour), and Energy Efficiency & Anomalies (flagging 1K inefficient intervals and isolating system performance spikes).
