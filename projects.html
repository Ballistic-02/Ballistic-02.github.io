07 Business Intelligence

Chicken Republic Stockout Analysis Dashboard

A production-grade 5-page Power BI dashboard analysing stockout patterns, demand behaviour, and stocking efficiency across 5 Chicken Republic branches in Lagos (Ajah, Lekki, Ikeja, Surulere, and Yaba). The dashboard was built to give operations and supply chain teams actionable visibility into where, when, and why stockouts occur and what it costs the business.

Key findings: an overall stockout rate of 0.22, ₦12M in total lost revenue, and 401 stockout days across the dataset. Ajah is the worst-performing branch (0.29), Fried Chicken (Full) is the most frequently stocked-out item, and stockouts peak between hours 19–20. Saturday drives the highest sales volume, and promotions marginally reduce stockout rates (0.21 vs 0.22 without promo). Suggested opening stock of 119.59 units with a stock adjustment of -6.28 per cycle.

The 5 pages cover: Executive Stockout Summary, Stockout Overview, Demand Patterns, External Drivers (weather and promotions), and Stocking Efficiency, each with interactive slicers for branch, item, and date.

08 Business Intelligence

HVAC Energy Efficiency & Performance Analytics

An end-to-end Power BI dashboard transforming complex IoT sensor logs from a commercial HVAC chiller plant into actionable energy insights, helping facility managers identify inefficiencies, reduce operational costs, and optimise building cooling performance. Built on a star schema data model with custom DAX measures and a dark-mode visual layout.

Key metrics: 0.48 kW/RT average efficiency, 1.73M kWh total energy consumption, 520.94 RT average building load, and a 10% inefficient rate across 14K logged intervals with anomaly spikes reaching 1.51 kW/RT. The 4 pages cover: Executive Summary (baseline KPIs and multi-year trend analysis), Weather Impact (efficiency vs. outdoor temperature and humidity), Time Pattern Analysis (hourly, daily, and weekday/weekend load behaviour), and Energy Efficiency & Anomalies (flagging 1K inefficient intervals and isolating system performance spikes).

09 ML + Business Intelligence

Predictive Maintenance Analytics (Industrial Failure Prediction)

An end-to-end predictive maintenance system bridging mechanical engineering domain knowledge with machine learning, simulating a physics-informed sensor fleet of 19 industrial units and building a failure-prediction pipeline that flags at-risk equipment 7 days before failure. Results feed a 4-page Power BI dashboard designed for fleet managers and maintenance planners.

The synthetic dataset isn't random noise, it's built on realistic degradation physics: vibration and temperature climbing on an accelerating curve in the weeks before failure, current draw rising with mechanical resistance, pump pressure dropping under cavitation. 41,363 sensor readings across 4 equipment types, 8 failure modes, and an 18-month simulation window. A Random Forest Classifier with time-aware train/test split (no data leakage) achieves ROC-AUC 0.9985, 97% recall, and catches 799 of 825 pre-failure windows in the test set. Top features: current vibration level, 3-day vibration slope, and rolling vibration volatility consistent with real-world condition monitoring literature.

The Power BI dashboard covers: Fleet Overview (KPIs, risk distribution, top-5 at-risk units), Equipment Deep Dive (live sensor trends with danger-zone lines per unit), Failure Risk & Predictions (fleet risk ranking matrix and failure mode breakdown), and Maintenance Impact (MTBF and cost avoidance from predictive vs. reactive strategy).
